## Part 1 — Welcome & Setup (5 min)

> *Read aloud:*

"Thank you for taking the time today. We're testing a new feature in the Academic Scheduler — we want to understand how easy or difficult it is to use, not how well you perform. There are no right or wrong answers.

Please think out loud as you go — tell us what you're looking at, what you're expecting, what's confusing. We're testing the product, not you.

I may not answer questions during tasks, but I'll note them and we can discuss at the end. Any questions before we start?"

---

## Part 2 — Background Questions (5 min)

1. How long have you been working as a campus manager?
2. Walk me through how you currently set up the timetable at the start of a semester — what does that process look like?
3. How many programs and batches do you typically schedule?
4. How do you handle mid-semester timetable changes today?
5. Do you use Excel or Google Sheets for timetable work currently?

---

## Part 3 — Tasks (30 min)

> *Hand over prototype. Do not explain the feature — observe.*

---

### Task 1 — First Impression (3 min)
**Setup:** Prototype is open. Import side sheet is visible.

> "You've been asked to load this semester's timetable into the scheduler. Take a look at this screen and tell me what you'd do."

**Observe:**
- Do they understand Fresh vs Modify without explanation?
- Do they notice the download template option?
- Any hesitation or confusion on the mode cards?

**Probing questions:**
- "What does 'Fresh Timetable' mean to you?"
- "When would you use 'Modify Timetable'?"

---

### Task 2 — Download & Configure Template (8 min)
**Setup:** Fresh Timetable mode selected.

> "You want to download the template to fill in your timetable. Go ahead and do that."

**Observe:**
- Do they find the download button easily?
- Do they understand the slot configuration fields?
- Do they try to configure per-program, or stick to global?
- Do they understand slots before/after lunch and lunch duration?

**Probing questions:**
- "Is the information here enough for you to configure correctly, or would you need to check something first?"
- "If one program has a different slot structure, what would you do here?"
- "Does this match how your campus actually structures the day?"

---

### Task 3 — Upload & Review (8 min)
**Setup:** Pre-filled sample template ready to upload.

> "Here's a filled timetable file. Upload it and tell me what you see."

**Observe:**
- Do they understand the parsed summary?
- Do they read the sheet table or skip it?
- How do they react to warnings/errors?
- Do they trust the data or want to cross-check?

**Probing questions:**
- "Looking at this screen, are you confident the file was read correctly?"
- "If you saw a warning here, what would you do next?"
- "Is there anything you expected to see here that you don't?"

---

### Task 4 — Modify Flow & Effective Date (6 min)
**Setup:** Switch to Modify Timetable mode, upload a diff file.

> "It's mid-semester and you need to update the timetable for two programs — some slots have changed. Use this file to do that."

**Observe:**
- Do they find the Effective From option?
- Do they choose Tomorrow or a custom date — and why?
- Do they review the before/after diff or skip it?
- Are they confident to submit after seeing the diff?

**Probing questions:**
- "When you make a timetable change, how far in advance do you typically plan it?"
- "Looking at the before/after changes here, is this enough for you to confirm you're applying the right update?"
- "Would you need anyone else's sign-off before hitting Submit?"

---

### Task 5 — Bulk Scheduling (5 min)
**Setup:** After confirming import, bulk scheduling screen is shown.

> "The file has been confirmed. Now take a look at this screen and tell me what's happening."

**Observe:**
- Do they understand the per-program status cards?
- Do they know what to do if one program shows an error?
- Do they feel in control or anxious during the scheduling run?

**Probing questions:**
- "If one program fails here, what would you want to happen to the others?"
- "After everything shows as done, what's the first thing you'd check?"

---

## Part 4 — Wrap-up Questions (5 min)

1. On a scale of 1–5, how confident did you feel using this feature for the first time?
2. What was the most confusing part of the process?
3. What felt smooth or natural?
4. Is there anything in your current timetable process that this feature doesn't cover but should?
5. Would you use this feature to set up the timetable at the start of next semester? Why or why not?
